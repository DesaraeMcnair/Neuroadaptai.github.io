Founder Story

The idea for Neuro Adapt AI began with my daughter.

When she first received her AAC device, I felt hopeful because technology was giving her another way to communicate. At the same time, I experienced the challenge many families face when introducing new assistive technology.

Before the device, my daughter and I built our own communication system through connection. She communicated through gestures, showing me what she needed, routines, and behaviors that I learned to understand over time.

As I watched her interact with technology, I began asking bigger questions:

  • What if technology could learn her the same way I was learning her?
  • What if technology could recognize patterns?
  • What if technology could adapt as she grows?

That question became the foundation of Neuro Adapt AI.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To develop adaptive artificial intelligence systems that learn individual patterns and personalize support to improve communication, learning, independence, and quality of life.

Vision

To create a future where technology understands human differences and adapts to support every person's ability to learn, communicate, recover, and thrive.
